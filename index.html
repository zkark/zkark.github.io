About me

I am Zain Soliman, a robotics engineer from Syria, based in Budapest. I completed a five-year B.Eng. in Robotics and Intelligent Systems at Manara University in 2025, with a Very Good mention in a selective cohort of 15 students. My graduation project, MirrorGlove, was a hand exoskeleton controlled by mirroring a therapist’s hand, built by a team of four, where I developed all the software, including the stereo-camera 3D hand pose estimation. I also ranked 34th out of 192 in a Kaggle chest X-ray competition, classifying 14 conditions with a ConvNeXt-based deep learning pipeline. In a quadcopter project, I built a MATLAB/Simulink pipeline and benchmarked Simple Adaptive Control against PID, LQR and LQI under model mismatch. Alongside my studies I worked as a private engineering tutor and volunteered as an English tutor with the ENGin program.

At the moment I am an MSc student in Computer Science for Autonomous Systems at Eötvös Loránd University, studying on the Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship. My main abilities are Python, PyTorch and TensorFlow, computer vision and deep learning, and control and simulation in MATLAB/Simulink, together with C/C++, ROS and Git. I am interested in robot autonomy, especially surgical robotics, medical imaging, and perception and control for autonomous systems. I enjoy working in teams. I speak Arabic natively and English at C1 level, and I am learning Hungarian and have a basic level of French.

I am looking for part-time, internship or research opportunities where I can apply these skills in robotics and AI. My long-term goal is to increase the autonomy of robots in the operating room. To get there, I plan to finish my MSc in spring 2028 and pursue research and engineering work on perception and control for autonomous surgical systems.

Projects

MirrorGlove: hand exoskeleton with therapist-mirroring control

Graduation project, team of 4, January 2024 to August 2025

I developed all the software: stereo-camera 3D hand pose estimation (MediaPipe, MMPose) that drives the exoskeleton, plus a comparison of hand pose models on accuracy versus computational efficiency. With my mechanical teammates I helped build a prototype from scratch and turned its friction and encoder-resolution limits into the next design iteration.

Code on GitHub

Chest X-ray classification

Kaggle competition, rank 34 of 192, 2025, two-person team

Multi-label classification of 14 chest-disease conditions from X-ray images. I built the Python training pipeline: ConvNeXt with a label-attention head, asymmetric focal loss for class imbalance, patient-wise cross-validation and ensembling, reaching a leaderboard AUROC of 0.930.

Code on GitHub

Quadcopter trajectory tracking with Simple Adaptive Control

MATLAB/Simulink, 2023 to 2026

A reproducible MATLAB pipeline from trajectory generation and linearization to controller design, simulation and metrics. It benchmarks Simple Adaptive Control against PID, LQR and LQI under model mismatch, and is published as an open-source repository (MIT) with documented results and known limitations.
